# Private picker rubric

Score each criterion 0-4: (1) invalidation race and identity ownership; (2) completion-based TTL, failure retry and undefined correctness; (3) usage/sketch agreement with small deep public API; (4) boundary validation and deterministic testability; (5) maintainability, structural alternatives and red-flag screening. Prefer fewer independently synchronized states when tied. A stale-write or stale-pending-join defect disqualifies until revised.
